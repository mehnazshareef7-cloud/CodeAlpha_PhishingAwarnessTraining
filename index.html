🛡️ Phishing Awareness Training

Learn how to identify phishing attacks and stay safe online

📌 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where hackers try to steal sensitive information such as passwords, bank details, and OTPs using fake emails, websites, or messages.

📧 How to Recognize Phishing Emails

  • Unknown sender email address
  • Urgent messages asking for quick action
  • Spelling and grammar mistakes
  • Suspicious links and attachments
  • Fake login pages

🌐 Fake Website Detection

  • Check website spelling carefully
  • Look for HTTPS security
  • Avoid unknown websites
  • Never enter passwords on suspicious pages

🧠 Social Engineering Tactics

Attackers manipulate people emotionally using fear, urgency, fake rewards, and fake customer support messages.

✅ Best Practices

  • Use strong passwords
  • Enable two-factor authentication
  • Never share OTPs
  • Verify suspicious emails
  • Update antivirus regularly
